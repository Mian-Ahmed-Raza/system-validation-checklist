# Validation Checklist for the MediTrack SRS

CityCare Clinic has asked us, as consultants, to check the requirements document (SRS) for MediTrack, an online appointment system, before they sign the contract.

## What we did

1. Built a checklist of 20 yes/no items (checklist v0.1).
2. Each of us answered the checklist alone on the SRS.
3. Compared our answers and listed the real problems in the document.

## Folder structure

system-validation-checklist/
├── srs/
├── checklist/
├── runs/
├── findings.md
└── README.md

## Key results

- 14 problems found in the SRS (see `findings.md`).
- Main issues: contradiction between R-04 and R-05, vague wording ("quickly", "securely", "user-friendly"), no emergency or elderly support, and no requirements for the administrator or data protection officer.
